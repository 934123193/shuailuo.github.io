## Research Areas

### Forensic Age Estimation

Research on adult skeletal imaging, bone-density features, and multi-method age assessment, with emphasis on quantitative applications of multidetector CT in forensic age estimation.

### Medical Imaging and AI

Work on CT/MRI-based measurement, medical image semantic segmentation, three-dimensional deep learning frameworks, and automated quantitative indicator calculation.

### LLM-assisted Research Workflows

Exploration of DeepSeek, Qwen, and related model APIs for literature organization, structured data extraction, research scripting, and reproducible experiment records.
