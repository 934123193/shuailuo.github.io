## 01 / About

I work at the intersection of forensic medicine, medical imaging, and artificial intelligence. My research focuses on forensic age estimation, quantitative CT/MRI analysis, automated imaging biomarkers, and deep learning-assisted assessment methods for forensic and medical applications.

My representative first-author work includes adult age estimation using pubic bone mineral density from multidetector CT, interpretable deep learning for automated dental age estimation from panoramic radiographs, gestational age estimation from multi-view fetal brain MRI, age assessment of Chinese Tibetan twins, and automated pneumothorax collapse quantification using deep learning semantic segmentation.

I am also interested in applying large language model APIs, including DeepSeek and Qwen, to literature organization, structured data extraction, research scripting, reproducible experiment records, and medical imaging research workflows.
