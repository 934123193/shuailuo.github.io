## 01 / ABOUT

I work at the intersection of forensic medicine, medical imaging, and artificial intelligence. My research focuses on forensic age estimation, CT/MRI-based quantitative imaging analysis, and automated assessment methods for forensic and medical applications.

My first-author work covers adult age estimation using pubic bone mineral density from multidetector CT, age assessment of Chinese Tibetan twins, gestational age estimation from fetal brain MRI measurements, and automated pneumothorax collapse quantification using deep learning semantic segmentation.

I am also interested in applying large language model APIs, including DeepSeek and Qwen, to research coding, literature extraction, structured data processing, and reproducible academic workflows.
